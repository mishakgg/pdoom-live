## Decision and measurement boundaries

Retain four producer families and five empirical collections: DebateGPT, Spitale, Ofcom (two separate surveys), and Costello. The frozen 64-source inventory and canonical contracts are unchanged. Read the [structured catalog](../../../data/evidence-program/research/persuasion-information.json) and [curated contrast ledger](../../../data/evidence-program/research/debategpt-correction-ledger.json).

The first useful implementation is a hand-curated published-effect ledger with synthetic regression tests. It validates two current DebateGPT comparisons and one superseded p-value assertion. It does not read participant CSVs, generate messages, fit statistical models or measure anyone’s persuasiveness.

Keep these evidence classes separate:

- Assigned laboratory exposure: immediate experimental attitude outcomes under a specified model and comparator.
- Controlled content judgments: participant correctness and authenticity classifications for selected items.
- Perceived population exposure: recalled, prompted survey responses, with routing, unknown recognition accuracy and survey uncertainty.
- Population causal impact: not established by this shortlist. No p(doom), completed real-world behavior, universal persuasion score or influence optimization is produced.

### Ranking

2. PI003, PI004: Population-survey context with separate definitions and substantial uncertainty.
3. PI002: Published classification outcomes; workbook recoding/access held.
