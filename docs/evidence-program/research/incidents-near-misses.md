## Evidence boundaries

An assertion needs its author, authority, evidence locator and artifact revision. A single event-wide verified flag loses essential distinctions. Product AI presence, engagement, learned-component causation, reported malfunction, intervention, potential near-miss, measured harm and legal remedy are different claims. No count here becomes a universal incident rate or p(doom). These are source descriptions, not legal or medical advice.

Manufacturer/provider accounts stay attributed. None reported, not measured and verified zero stay distinct. Repeated scans are not unique people; systems are not patients; requests are not harmed users. No names, VINs, precise locations, clinical particulars, patient/personnel files, leaked data or narrative corpus are retained.

## Small offline implementation

[read_nhtsa_corrections.py](../../../tools/evidence_program/read_nhtsa_corrections.py) validates a manually curated, attributed fact ledger for exactly two agency publication changes in May–September 2026. It is not a PDF parser or live collector. The source dictionary edition is September 15; the changes are June 15 and August 27; retrieval is October 8. Exactly one report-scoped correction and one release-wide correction are retained, with no omitted-report count invented.

The real dictionary hash identifies the privately acquired 529,128-byte agency PDF. It differs from the curated JSON hash; other source hashes stay null where original bytes were not acquired. No original source PDF/HTML is in the public payload. Source documentary acceptance, manual curation and synthetic/adversarial decoder tests are separate verification stages.

Stable assertion identity differs from artifact revision identity. A new artifact revision under the same report ID/version appends an observation without changing the correction into a new event. Repeated unchanged input is idempotent. Earlier content remains explicitly unavailable when not captured; neither a before-narrative nor a prior original-content hash is invented. Output excludes engagement, cause, injury, rates and any catastrophe probability.

The reader rejects unknown structure, duplicate keys, invalid types/dates, oversized or excess inputs and unsafe file paths; it does not silently truncate into an empty success. The fixture directory uses local LF attributes, with a real core.autocrlf=true Git checkout regression for byte portability. Existing fixture files and prior contracts remain unchanged.
