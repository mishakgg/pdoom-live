1. AlgoPerf v0.5: strongest controlled, inspectable and explicitly licensed bounded pilot.
2. NanoGPT optimization track 3: useful data-efficiency evidence, conditional on contributed-log rights and precise track/run metadata.
3. Epoch language-model progress: valuable historical synthesis, conditional on spreadsheet access, rights and version mapping.

A matched performance target is necessary but insufficient for one common efficiency curve. Workload, dataset, evaluation, resource regime, accounting rules, tuning search and statistical specification must remain compatible. This review establishes no universal efficiency curve and no measured energy time series. Optimizer and hyperparameters are treatment variables; they are not controls that must match across algorithms.

The local AlgoPerf analysis is not an official aggregate leaderboard score, an MLCommons endorsement or a new benchmark result submission. One trial supplies 74 checkpoints, not 74 independent replications. No GPU training, corpus/model/raw medical-image download or upstream source-code execution occurred.

- One trial is not an official aggregate score, a five-trial search result, a five-study median or a failure-rate estimate. Seventy-four checkpoints are not replicates.

- Optimizer and full hyperparameters are treatments. Workload/version, architecture, evaluation target, hardware/resource regime and timing rules are comparison constraints.
- Published approximately 28% speedup has failed-run budget substitution and omitted held-out invalidation qualifications; it is not an unconditional compute reduction.
- No paired baseline in the pilot: no efficiency ratio, energy estimate, FLOP inference, secular scaling rate or risk probability.
