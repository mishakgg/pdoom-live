# Cohort 2026.10.0

Cohort `cohort_2026_10` keeps every member of cohort `2026.09.0` and adds a small set of people from frontier labs that had no current member in that roster. It is a purposive seed. It is not a census of AI researchers, not a random sample, and not a measure of what the field believes.

The historical files in `data/seed/cohort/v2026-09/` are not edited. Membership changes are the diff in `data/seed/cohort/v2026-10/membership_diff.json`. The base commit is `d13c8ab9bc2d643e28b7f52daf1a4c15724e77d7`.

## Inclusion

New people use the same primary reasons as [cohort 2026.09.0](./COHORT_METHODOLOGY.md):

- `frontier_model_author` — named as an author of a frontier model on that lab's own papers or team page.
- `frontier_lab_researcher` — a research or research-leadership role at a lab that trains frontier models, stated on an official page.

Audience size is not a reason. A target headcount is not a reason. A person is omitted when the only page found is a chatbot share, a stale hiring title, or an executive role with no research duty on the official page.
